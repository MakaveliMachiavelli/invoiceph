FAQ

Do I need to issue invoices now instead of official receipts?

Yes — under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act and BIR RR 11-2024 (effective April 27, 2024), all sellers of goods and services, including freelancers, issue invoices. Official receipts are phased out for this purpose.

What must the invoice contain to be BIR-compliant?

Name, TIN and business address of seller; buyer's name, TIN and address; serial number and date of issue; quantity, unit of measure, description and unit price per line; and your VAT status — VAT-registered sellers show the 12% VAT, non-VAT sellers print "NON-VAT". (Formal BIR registration of your invoice series with the RDO still applies — this tool produces the document itself.)

I'm a freelancer earning under ₱3M — which mode do I pick?

Most likely Non-VAT / VAT-exempt. If you're VAT-registered, pick one of the VAT modes depending on whether your quoted prices already include 12% VAT.

Is my data uploaded?

No. There is no backend. Everything stays in your browser's local storage. You can clear it anytime with "New invoice".
