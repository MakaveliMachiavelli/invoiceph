How to Make a BIR-Compliant Invoice in 2026

Everything that changed with the Ease of Paying Taxes Act and BIR RR 11-2024 (effective April 27, 2024) — and exactly what your invoice needs. Or skip to the free generator that handles all of it.

1. The big change: invoices replace official receipts

Under the EOPT Act and RR 11-2024, sellers of both goods and services — including freelancers and self-employed professionals — now issue invoices. The old distinction (sales invoice for goods, official receipt for services) is gone. Existing ORs/SIs could still be used up to their 5-year validity or until June 30, 2025 for unregistered ones; today, new issuances must follow the invoice rules.

2. Required contents of a compliant invoice

  • Seller: registered name, TIN, and business address (or branch code)
  • Buyer: name, and TIN/address when applicable (buyer TIN optional for non-VAT customers under EOPT)
  • Document style: the word "INVOICE" prominently
  • Serial number (continuous, per establishment/branch) and date of issue
  • Per line: quantity, unit of measure, description, and unit price
  • VAT status: VAT-registered sellers must show the 12% VAT amount (per item or in aggregate); non-VAT sellers must print "NON-VAT"; VAT-exempt sales say "VAT EXEMPT"
  • Copies: original for the buyer; duplicate for the seller (that's your books)

Also standard practice: amount in words, and signature of the seller/authorized signatory.

3. VAT vs NON-VAT: which layout applies to you?

  • Non-VAT / VAT-exempt — most freelancers (8% or below ₱3M gross): print "NON-VAT", charge no VAT. Percentage tax (3%), where applicable, is borne by the seller and typically priced in.
  • VAT-registered (above ₱3M or voluntary): show "VAT-registered" and the 12% VAT — either prices are VAT-exclusive (VAT added on top) or VAT-inclusive (VAT carved out of the price). Both are allowed; the invoice must make the amount clear.

4. Do I need to register my invoices with the BIR?

Yes — invoice series generally need authority-to-issue (ATI) processing with your RDO for manually-issued invoices, and e-invoicing rules may apply to large taxpayers. This guide covers the document contents; for registration of your specific series, confirm with your RDO. Many freelancers issue computer-generated invoices from accredited formats — check current RDO practice.

5. Common 2026 mistakes that trigger notices

  • Still issuing "OFFICIAL RECEIPT" for services (outdated post-EOPT)
  • Missing "NON-VAT" label (non-VAT sellers)
  • No buyer TIN where required, or no per-line quantity/unit/price detail
  • Reusing serial numbers or skipping series after voiding one
